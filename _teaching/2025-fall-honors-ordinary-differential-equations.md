MATH-UA 268. Instructor: Professor Charles Peskin.

## Course overview

A rigorous introduction to the theory of ordinary differential equations, including existence and uniqueness for linear and nonlinear equations over the real and complex numbers. The course also examines qualitative behavior of solutions, connections to dynamical systems, and applications in physics and biology.
