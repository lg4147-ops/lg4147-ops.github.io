MATH-UA 144. Instructor: Professor Charles Peskin.

## Course overview

The course introduces mathematical modeling, numerical methods, and MATLAB programming through simulations of planetary and spacecraft motion, epidemic and endemic diseases, stringed musical instruments, and urban traffic. Animations, with sound where appropriate, are used to communicate the results. Professor Peskin's lecture notes also include the simulation of a computer.
