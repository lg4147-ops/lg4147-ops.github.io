最近用 AI 比较多，想记下一点自己的感受。至少从目前的使用经验来看，我没有觉得它那么可怕。我自己也订阅了 ChatGPT Pro，但用下来，很多时候还是会觉得：它好像也没有想象中那么聪明。

当然，AI 处理和利用现有材料的能力确实很强。整理信息、处理数据、写代码，以及把一些具体想法实现出来，它的速度常常比我快得多。这些帮助很实在。不过，对于它能不能提出真正有启发性的新思想，我目前的感受仍然比较有限。也许它可以，只是我还没有用到，或者还没有找到合适的使用方式。

我比较明确的一个体会是：当我自己已经有了想法，并且把目标、限制和细节交代清楚时，AI 通常能做得好很多。如果只是给它一个模糊的方向，让它自由发挥，它就很容易偏离我真正关心的问题。有时候，来回解释和纠正方向，本身也需要花不少精力。

最近也看到有人用 AI 做纯数学研究。我会好奇，这些工作里有多少来自已有知识的提炼和重新组合，有多少来自形式化环境中的搜索与验证，又有多少体现了新的数学想法。在仔细了解具体工作之前，我不想把这些都归为一类。我的一个猜测是，当问题能够被清楚地形式化，结果又有明确的验证标准时，AI 可能更容易发挥优势。

至于应用数学，至少在我自己接触的工作里，我对它的评价还比较保留。问题应该怎样建模，哪些假设合理，数值结果到底说明了什么，这些地方仍然需要自己反复思考。AI 能帮我推进很多具体步骤，但在决定该往哪里走这件事上，目前给我的惊喜还不算多。

当然，这些都只是我现阶段的使用感受。我也不想因为自己还没有见到，就断言 AI 不可能产生新的思想。Who knows，也许以后我会改变看法。目前我更愿意继续用它，也继续观察它究竟能帮我走到哪一步。

Some Personal Thoughts on AI

I have been using AI quite a bit lately and wanted to write down a few impressions. At least from my experience so far, I do not find it all that frightening. I subscribe to ChatGPT Pro, but I still often come away thinking that it does not seem quite as intelligent as I expected.

AI is certainly good at working with existing material. It can organize information, process data, write code, and turn concrete ideas into working implementations much faster than I can in many cases. That help is real. My experience with it producing genuinely insightful new ideas, however, has been more limited. Perhaps it can do so, and I simply have not encountered it yet—or have not found the right way to use it.

One thing has become fairly clear to me: AI usually works much better when I already have an idea and explain the goals, constraints, and details carefully. If I give it only a vague direction and let it proceed on its own, it can easily drift away from the question I actually care about. Explaining what I mean and repeatedly correcting its direction can take considerable effort, too.

I have also recently seen people using AI in pure mathematics research. I find myself wondering how much of this work comes from synthesizing and recombining existing knowledge, how much from search and verification within formal systems, and how much reflects new mathematical ideas. I would not want to put all these efforts into one category without examining the actual work. My tentative guess is that AI may have an advantage when a problem can be stated precisely and its proposed solutions checked against clear criteria.

As for applied mathematics, my assessment remains fairly reserved, at least within the work I have encountered. How should a problem be modeled? Which assumptions are reasonable? What do the numerical results actually tell us? These questions still require a great deal of thought on my part. AI helps me carry out many individual steps, but it has surprised me less when it comes to deciding which direction is worth pursuing.

Of course, these are only my impressions at this point. I would not conclude that AI cannot produce new ideas simply because I have not seen much of that myself. Who knows—perhaps I will change my mind. For now, I intend to keep using it and see how far it can help me go.
