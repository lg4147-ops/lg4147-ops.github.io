Information Theory for Statistics and Learning  
MATH-GA 2830-004 · Fall 2025 · Instructor: Yanjun Han

The course develops entropy, divergence, and mutual information as tools for statistics and learning. Applications include statistical lower bounds and questions in estimation, prediction, and sampling.

[Course Website](https://yanjunhan2021.github.io/courses/info_theory/index.html)

## Coursework

### Entropy and the Central Limit Theorem (CLT)

Course presentation by Liyan Gong and Shujun Xiong on the entropic central limit theorem, using relative entropy, Fisher information, and de Bruijn's identity.
