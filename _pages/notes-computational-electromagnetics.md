MATH-GA 2830-002 · Fall 2026 · Instructor: Mike O'Neil

This course studies electromagnetic wave propagation through the PDE and integral-equation formulations of Maxwell's equations. It connects the underlying theory with numerical methods for time-domain and high-frequency problems and propagation in complex geometries. Topics include time-harmonic fields, fast multipole methods, scattering, radiation conditions, and layered media.
