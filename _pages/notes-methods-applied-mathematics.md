MATH-GA 2701-001 · Fall 2025 · Instructor: Oliver Bühler

The course studies scaling and approximation methods for mathematical models, including regular and singular perturbation, multiple scales, and matched asymptotic expansions. Wave propagation connects these methods with Fourier analysis and geometric optics.

[Official course description](https://math.nyu.edu/dynamic/courses/graduate-course-descriptions/as/fall-2025/)

## Homework 6: Water Waves and Dispersion

Can the wave periods arriving at a beach reveal when and where a distant storm occurred? This assignment uses phase and group velocities to investigate deep-water waves, finite-depth and tsunami limits, and capillary–gravity waves generated by a localized disturbance.
