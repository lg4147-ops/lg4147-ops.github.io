MATH-GA 2710-001 · Fall 2026 · Instructor: Esteban Tabak

This graduate course explores connections between physical and mathematical thinking through the Lagrangian and Hamiltonian formulations of classical mechanics. It links particle and continuous-field dynamics with dispersive waves, the calculus of variations, and partial differential equations, and introduces relativity and the foundations of statistical and quantum mechanics through unifying mathematical principles.
